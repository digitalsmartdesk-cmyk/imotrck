Our Mission

Built for the emotional
journey of childhood

We believe every child deserves to be understood — not just achieved. ImoTracker gives families a shared language for emotions and a way to watch that language grow over time.

Try the free check-in

“Academic achievement gets measured constantly. Emotional growth barely gets measured at all. We wanted to change that.”

— Ravi Menon, Co-founder

What we believe

Three ideas at our core

01

Emotions need language

Children who can name their feelings are better equipped to manage them. Emotional literacy is a learnable skill — and it starts with vocabulary.

02

Parents are the key

Therapists and teachers play a role, but parents are the constant. Our job is to give parents insight and language, not replace their judgment.

03

Data over time reveals what a moment cannot

One snapshot tells you little. Eight years of annual check-ins reveals patterns, growth, and the quiet shifts that matter most.

The science

8 domains. One picture.

Each assessment tracks these eight areas of emotional development, drawn from decades of child psychology research.

1

Emotional Awareness

Can your child name and notice their feelings in the moment?

2

Emotional Regulation

How effectively can your child manage their emotional reactions?

3

Stress & Resilience

How does your child handle pressure and bounce back from setbacks?

4

Empathy & Social Sense

Does your child understand and care about others' feelings?

5

Relationships & Trust

How does your child build and maintain meaningful relationships?

6

Self-Perception

How does your child see themselves — strengths, values, identity?

7

Identity & Authenticity

Is your child developing a confident, authentic sense of self?

8

Meaning & Purpose

Does your child have a sense of what matters to them in life?
